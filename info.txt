### 1. Meaning of the Number Results:
 * **Mean Absolute Error (3.4):** This means that the marks predicted by your model can, on average, be ahead or behind the actual marks by 3.4 marks.
 **Mean Squared Error (20.74) & RMSE (4.55):** This looks at larger errors. It means that in some places, the error is getting a bit larger (up to approximately 4.5 marks).

 **R2 Score (0.0096):** This is the main point! Its value is very close to 0. This means that predicting a student's final marks solely by looking at "Study Time" (study hours) is simply not reliable. There are many other factors besides study time that have an impact (such as attendance, past records, health, etc.)
